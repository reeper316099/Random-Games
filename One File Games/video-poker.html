How to play

Pick a bet (1–5 credits per hand) and press Deal. You get five cards. Click the cards you want to hold (keys 1–5), then press Draw to replace the rest — once.

Your final hand is paid by the table above (Jacks or Better 9/6: a pair of Jacks or higher is the lowest paying hand). Betting the maximum 5 credits pays 4000 for a Royal Flush instead of 800 × bet.

After a win you can try to double it: guess whether the next card is higher or lower than the dealer's card. Ties push.
