How to play Crazy Eights

Get rid of all your cards first. On your turn play a card that matches the suit or rank of the top discard. Eights are wild: play one any time and name the next suit.

Can't play? Draw from the stock (either until you can play, or just one card then pass; pick it in the settings).

Action cards (optional): a 2 makes the next player draw two and miss a turn, a Q skips the next player, an A reverses direction.

When someone goes out, they score the cards left in the others' hands: 8 = 50, K Q J 10 = 10, A = 1, other cards their number. First to the target wins.
