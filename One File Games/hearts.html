How to play Hearts

Four players. Avoid taking points: every ♥ is 1 point and the Queen of Spades (♠Q) is 13. The player with the lowest score when someone reaches the target wins.

Each hand starts by passing 3 cards (left, right, across, then no pass). The player with the 2♣ leads it. You must follow suit if you can. Nobody can lead a ♥ until a heart has been played ("broken"), and no points can be played on the first trick unless you have nothing else.

Shooting the moon: take every heart and the Queen of Spades in one hand and everybody else gets +26 (or you get −26, depending on the setting).
