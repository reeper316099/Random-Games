🤥 BS (Cheat)

Get rid of all your cards — lie about what you play, and catch the others lying
Wins 0
Opponents Bots Rank rule
Your hand — 0 cards

How to play BS

The whole deck is dealt out. The goal is to be the first player with no cards.

On your turn you play 1–4 cards face down and say what they are — e.g. “two Queens”. Each turn has a required rank (A, 2, 3 … K in order, or the round starter's pick), but you can lie about what you actually play.

After a play, anyone may shout BS! The cards are turned over: if the player was lying they take the whole pile; if they were telling the truth the caller takes the pile.

Going out only counts if nobody catches you on your last play.
