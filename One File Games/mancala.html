How to play Mancala (Kalah)

Your six pits are the bottom row and your store is on the right. The bot's pits are on top, its store on the left. Pick one of your pits and its stones are dropped one at a time counter-clockwise (to the right on your row), through your store but skipping the bot's.

Extra turn: if your last stone lands in your store, you go again.

Capture: if your last stone lands in one of your own empty pits and the pit opposite has stones, you take them plus your last stone into your store (can be turned off).

The game ends when one side has no stones in its pits; the other side keeps what is left in its pits. Most stones in the store wins. Keys 1–6 play your pits left to right.
