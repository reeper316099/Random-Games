How to play Cribbage

  • Each hand: both players get 6 cards and throw 2 into the crib, which belongs to the dealer. A starter card is cut (a Jack gives the dealer 2, "his heels").
  • The play: starting with the non-dealer, take turns laying cards and calling the running count (face cards 10, Ace 1). It can't go over 31. You score for a count of 15 or 31 (2 each), pairs (2 / 6 / 12), runs of 3 or more (1 per card), Go and the last card (1 each).
  • The show: each player scores their 4-card hand plus the starter: fifteens (2 each), pairs (2), runs (1 per card), a flush (4, or 5 with the starter; the crib needs all 5) and nobs (a Jack matching the starter's suit, 1). The non-dealer counts first, then the dealer's hand, then the crib.
  • First to the target (121 or 61) wins. The board scores everything for you, and the breakdown is shown.
