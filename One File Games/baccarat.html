How to play Baccarat

Bet on which hand will finish closest to 9: the Player, the Banker or a Tie. Aces count 1, 2–9 their number, 10 and picture cards 0. A hand's value is its total mod 10 (a 7 and a 8 make 5).

Two cards each are dealt. A total of 8 or 9 is a natural and ends the round. Otherwise the Player draws on 0–5 and stands on 6–7, and the Banker follows the fixed third-card table. Nothing is up to you after the bets.
