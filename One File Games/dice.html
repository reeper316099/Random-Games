📖 How the bets work
  • Pass Line — placed before a "come-out" roll (puck OFF). 7 or 11 wins straight away; 2, 3 or 12 loses. Any other total becomes the point (puck ON) and the bet stays up: roll the point again to win, roll a 7 first and it loses. Pays 1:1.
  • Don't Pass — the opposite: 2 or 3 wins on the come-out, 12 is a tie, 7 or 11 loses. Once a point is set, a 7 wins and the point loses. Pays 1:1.
  • Under 7 / Over 7 — one roll, pays 1:1. A 7 loses both.
  • Lucky 7 — one roll, pays 4:1.
  • Field — one roll: 3, 4, 9, 10 or 11 pays 1:1, a 2 pays 2:1 and a 12 pays 3:1.
  • Any Doubles pays 4:1 • Any Craps (2, 3 or 12) pays 7:1 • Eleven pays 15:1 • Snake Eyes (1+1) and Boxcars (6+6) pay 30:1.
  • Right-click (or use Undo) to take a chip back. Pass and Don't Pass are locked while the point is on.
