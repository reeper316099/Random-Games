How to play Spades

You and Max (across from you) are a team against Ava and Zoe. ♠ Spades are always trump. Each hand, everyone bids how many tricks they expect to win. Your team's bids add up to your contract.

Follow suit if you can; otherwise you may trump with a spade. You can't lead a spade until one has been played ("broken"), unless you only hold spades. The highest spade wins; with no spades, the highest card of the led suit wins.

Scoring: make your contract for 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 per extra trick (a "bag" — ten bags cost 100 points). Miss it and lose 10 per trick bid. A Nil bid (win zero tricks) is worth +100 if you pull it off and −100 if you don't.
