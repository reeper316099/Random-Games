Remorse Chess rules

Normal chess — checkmate the king to win — plus a reaction system. The first time a piece captures, a D6 decides how it feels (each piece rolls once and keeps that reaction):

  • 1 – Devastated 😭: frozen. A bishop must absolve it within 3 turns or it removes itself.
  • 2–3 – Guilt-ridden 😔: still moves, but refuses to capture until absolved.
  • 4–5 – Unaffected: plays normally.
  • 6 – Psychopath 😈: gets an extra move after a capture (no stacking). After 3 captures: bloodlust 🩸 — only moves to capture, even friendly pieces.

Absolution: move a bishop to a square next to a guilty/devastated friendly piece to clear it (this is your turn). A guilty bishop can absolve itself — select it and press “Absolve self”. Bishops roll too.

Witnesses: when a piece is captured, each surviving neighbour of the victim rolls: 1–2 temporary guilt (already guilty → devastated), 3–4 nothing, 5–6 vengeance ⚪ — one guilt-free capture of the same type of piece that made the kill.

Everything is adjustable in ⚙ Rule settings (presets: Orthodox chess, Full Remorse, Lite).
